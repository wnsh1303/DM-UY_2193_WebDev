Soft Scrambled Eggs

A light, creamy breakfast side dish

Instructions

  1. Crack 3 eggs into a bowl, add 1/2 cup milk.
  2. Add 1/3 tbsp sugar and 1/3 tbsp salt. Whisk well.
  3. Melt 2 tbsp butter in a pan over medium heat.
  4. Pour in egg mixture; gently push from edges to center.
  5. Remove from heat while still slightly moist. Top with black pepper.

Ingredients

  • 3 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1/3 tbsp sugar
  • 1/3 tbsp salt
  • Black pepper
